The Chocolate Shop

Grandma Pearl left you her famous chocolate shop — and her strangest rule: no rulers allowed. Every customer orders an exact fraction, and you cut by eye alone.

1. Slice: hold down and drag the knife across the slab — wiggly cuts welcome, as long as each slice goes clean through. One piece per customer.
2. Match: drag each customer's badge onto their piece.
3. Serve: every slice reveals its TRUE percent, and closer cuts mean bigger tips.
4. Grow: spend tips on shop upgrades. Level up. Bigger parties. Trickier slabs. Chocolate pizza wheels.
