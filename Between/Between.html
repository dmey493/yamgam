Between

One daily puzzle. Five fractions. No repeats possible.

1. Name a fraction between the gold number and the teal number.
2. Your answer becomes the new bottom. Find one between it and the top.
3. Do it five times. The line zooms in — you'll never run out of room.
4. Each round has a denominator budget: ≤12, then ≤24, ≤36, ≤48, ≤60. No hiding behind /100!

Strategy is yours: common denominators, decimal scouting, or the sneaky add-the-tops-add-the-bottoms move. The smaller your denominators, the more elegance points you earn.
