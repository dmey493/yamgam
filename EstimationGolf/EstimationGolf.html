How to play

The pin 🚩 out there is a target number, and your ball is a number. Swing operation clubs to close the gap — each swing is 1 stroke and each club works once. There are no previews: estimate what each club will do, then watch the flag walk closer (or vanish into the haze). When you stop:
in the cupholed! no putts — the ball drops in by itself on the green+1 putt fairway+2 (chip on, then putt) deep rough+3 — it happens to everyone
Every hole has a line into the cup — find it and you're under par. Watch out: below zero is water 💦 — that swing costs a stroke, the club goes back in the bag, and you play from where you were. Stuck? A practice swing shows exactly where one club would land, but costs a stroke.
